Good interfaces keep your place.

I'm building Focus Trail: a small tool that makes keyboard focus changes reviewable in Playwright tests.

Real keypresses. Authored checkpoints. A reviewed baseline. An offline report that shows the first changed step.

Swipe for a controlled demo: the dialog closes, but focus doesn't return to its opener.

Pilot candidate v0.1.0. Source and demo instructions:
